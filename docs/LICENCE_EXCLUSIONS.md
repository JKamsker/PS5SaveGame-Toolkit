# Licence exclusions and missing rights evidence

Central register for #37, checked against `main` on 2026-10-28. It consolidates all 17 plugin
READMEs, their `*.source.md` sidecars, third-party notices (hyphenated and underscored names) and
`EVIDENCE.md` files. Linked local records explain the scope; upstream URLs identify the material.
This is a record of the evidence those files contain, not a fresh determination of upstream terms.
`none found` means no applicable grant was established in the recorded investigation, not proof
that the author cannot grant one. Unpinned sources explicitly say so. Unknown licence versions are
not filled in by guesswork.

The [owner policy in plugin-guide rule 9](PLUGIN_GUIDE.md#hard-rules) permits MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0,
LGPL, GPL-2.0-or-later, GPL-3.0 and CC BY / CC BY-SA with attribution and required terms. The only
licence refusal categories are GPL-2.0-only code, no licence/all rights reserved, NC/no-redistribution
terms and proprietary game assets. No reviewed plugin record identifies a specific GPL-2.0-only
source refusal. Merely choosing an independent implementation of permitted code is not a licence
refusal. A compatible tool licence also does not automatically license its extracted game assets.

For each refusal, keep one row per source here and in the plugin README's `## Licence exclusions`
table. Retain exact source/revision, material refused, alternative and a practical unblocking step.
The second table records requested missing-pack/artwork cases separately: lack of matching data,
access or a semantic join must not be mislabeled as an incompatible licence.

## Maintaining the register

Update both tables when a source is licensed, replaced or its refusal reason changes. Record the
new revision, scope of the grant and retained notices before embedding. An owner can ask the
author/rights holder for compatible terms or provide a permitted offline pack; private ownership
of a game copy alone does not establish redistribution permission.

Research-only mentions of permitted code/data are not exclusions: examples include GPL-3.0
WolvenKit/KingdomSaveEditor, MIT PlayStation-PS1HD/Nex/neuro-pragmata and the adopted CC BY-SA wiki
catalogues. Insomniac's EUPL/GPL research notes record independent implementation, not an established
licence refusal under the owner's categories. Access errors, unsupported platforms, missing save
identities and incomplete dependency writers remain separate technical/evidence limits.

Follow the [owner checklist](OWNER_CHECKLIST.md) for native captures and console validation and
report a resolved rights entry on [#37](https://github.com/JKamsker/PS5SaveGame-Toolkit/issues/37)
or its linked plugin issue without posting private game dumps.
