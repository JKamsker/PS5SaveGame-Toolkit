# SuckerPunch — improvement plan

> **Historical planning snapshot — 2026-10-28.** The baseline, proposed work, test counts and
> policy restrictions below describe that research round, not current implementation or permissions.
> See [current plugin status](../PLUGINS.md), the linked plugin README,
> [delivered framework features](ROADMAP.md) and [current policy](../PLUGIN_GUIDE.md#hard-rules).

## Summary

The `suckerpunch` 0.2.0 plugin already has a preservation-focused, Experimental Tsushima resource/ammo editor; Yōtei is an opaque read-only inspector with useful research tests. A substantially better editor would provide named resources, a character/progression sheet, owned equipment and charm loadouts, technique prerequisites, and a region-based collectible tracker, with icons and descriptions when their provenance permits. The three main blockers are **controlled PS5 saves and console validation**, **proven save-ID/ownership/progression mappings plus usable reference-data rights**, and **Yōtei's unresolved integrity coverage and authoritative gameplay records**. Existing SDK tables, enums, actions, assets and multi-file outputs can deliver substantial early improvements; rich pickers, maps, user-supplied data packs and structural undo need targeted framework work. Reads should ship before new writes, existing records before creation, and each title/build/mode should retain its own evidence and capability gates.

### Integrity / crypto

- **Tsushima, verified on this corpus:** checksum at `0x08` is `(sum(bytes[0x10:contentEnd]) + contentEnd + 0x14E + version) mod 2^32`, using end at `0x04` and version at `0x0C`. All 25 payloads match. Input is already decrypted; no additional key is required for the observed inner edits. This neither provides PS5 outer-container decryption/resigning nor proves the absence of other consistency dependencies for future equipment/story edits. Preserve header/platform words, unknown bytes and padding.
- **Platform caution, verified locally:** in all 15 v49 entries, the converter's known build word appears at `0x188`, its PS4-labelled marker at `0x190`, with another 8-byte word at `0x180`; the 8 v48 entries have that build word at `0x188` but a different next word. These are structural observations, not newly established platform meanings. v47 is different again. Conversion rules require matched PC/PS4/PS5 captures, not a version switch.
- **Yōtei, hypothesis only:** word at `0x0C` matches `(sum(bytes[0x10:end]) + end + 0x150) mod 2^32`, with end from `0x08`, in both non-empty payloads. The 13/16 word at `0x04` is not added; its meaning is unknown. Every byte after end is zero, so content-only and whole-file sums produce the same result. Additional zero-padded samples alone cannot settle coverage. An authoritative implementation, a naturally non-zero tail, or a controlled private mutation/load/resave experiment is needed; synthesizing a non-zero tail and recomputing our own hypothesis is not proof.
- No usable Yōtei inner crypto/key/signature implementation was located. That is an evidence gap, not evidence of encryption. Do not rename the 13/16 field “version,” enforce a speculative checksum on imports, or enable writes merely because the additive relation fits. Confirm authoritative quantities and any sibling/profile state independently of integrity first.

## Vision

- **Overview / character:** game/build/mode, selected slot, save-generation provenance, inferred time/difficulty and proven legend/XP, health/resolve or Yōtei spirit. Show missing evidence and supported edit capabilities per document. Separate metadata summaries from live state and derived runtime stats.
- **Resources & ammo:** friendly name beside internal ID, category, icon/description when available, count, capacity rule and source/confidence. Controls include “refill unlocked ammo to proven capacity,” “set selected supported resources to target” and conservative upgrade-budget presets. Unknown-capacity records retain manual Experimental editing with the existing caveat; no misleading “max all” default.
- **Equipment & charms:** owned armor, masks/headgear, sword kits, dyes and weapon upgrades; equipped slots and armor-specific loadouts; six charm positions with major/minor/slot restrictions where verified. Search by name, ID and category, compare descriptions, apply presets only using owned eligible gear. Display catalogue presence separately from ownership and currently equipped state.
- **Techniques:** Samurai/Stances/Ghost groups, prerequisites, spent/unspent points and source unlocks. Read-only first; later grant/reset only proven ordinary purchasable techniques through atomic dependency-aware operations. Mythic/story techniques require their own state transition evidence.
- **World / journal:** per-region Tsushima/Iki lists for shrines, fox dens, hot springs, haiku, bamboo strikes, pillars, artifacts and quests; Yōtei uses its own region/activity dictionary. Distinguish discovered, visited, completed and rewarded. A list ships before map art/coordinates; a map highlights missing activities only after marker IDs and coordinate transforms are known. Marking an activity complete must not imply its reward was granted.
- **Changes / research:** readable before/after values and affected dependencies, undo/redo, evidence/provenance details, raw IDs and bounded raw inspection. Yōtei shows the candidate header/tables with explicit uncertainty and remains read-only until its separate gates pass.

## Phased plan

**Phase 1 — Better meaning on the proven Tsushima editor. Goal: immediate, low-risk usefulness. Size M; risk low to medium.**

- [ ] Establish a versioned evidence manifest from the 12 distinct Tsushima snapshots; record each identifier, structural evidence, sample hashes privately and observed vs. approved range separately. Replace broad semantic guesses with a reviewed title/build/mode allowlist.
- [ ] Obtain before/after counts and menu observations for Supplies, Flowers, Hide and each proposed `Ingred...` label; produce an attributed, small reference table with unresolved IDs retained.
- [ ] Add category/name/capacity-source columns and separate safety/confidence columns using existing nodes; improve visible table confidence/warnings in the shared UI workstream.
- [ ] Add an atomic target-quantity action for explicitly supported unlocked records; a capacity refill stays disabled where its upgrade/mode-dependent cap is unproved. Reuse existing checksum, mirror and scalar undo behavior.
- [ ] Console-load small resource/ammo edits, consume/buy one item, save again and compare; preserve the Experimental label until a human reviews the evidence.

Evidence needed: R1/R2/R4/R6/R7/R11 below; existing saves establish structural preservation, not friendly-label truth or capacity. Exit: a trustworthy named resource editor, even without icons or new SDK nodes.

**Phase 2 — Read equipment and character/progression relationships. Goal: a useful owned-gear browser and character sheet. Size L; risk medium/high.**

- [ ] Build bounded readers around actual counted/length-prefixed structures near observed equipment/stance identifiers; locate independent boundaries rather than assuming five-word inventory records apply elsewhere.
- [ ] Prove catalogue vs. owned vs. equipped records, stable identities, gear/charm duplicates and active vs. per-armor loadouts. Preserve record spans and unknown bytes. Never use string presence as ownership.
- [ ] Obtain original gear-hash research and/or user-owned definitions; join internal ID→definition→localization/icon key with exact build/locale and ambiguous mapping rejection.
- [ ] Read legend/XP, technique earned/spent state, stances, upgrades, base health/resolve and capacity dependencies only when single-action captures support the interpretation. Identify derived vs. stored quantities.
- [ ] Ship lazy read-only equipment/technique tables first; enrich with permitted icons and searchable pickers as shared capabilities arrive. Unknown or unsupported builds stay opaque/read-only in new sections.

Evidence needed: R1/R2/R3/R6/R8/R9/R11/R12. Risk: mirrored/state-cache/dictionary records resemble live data; runtime CE structures may not serialize directly. Exit: known-state PS5 menu comparisons for every displayed curated field.

**Phase 3 — Constrained equipment, technique and character edits. Goal: change existing supported state safely. Size L; risk high.**

- [ ] Start with existing owned gear selection and proven scalar upgrade/point fields; determine whether selections are fixed-width references or variable-length strings. Variable-size replacements wait for Phase 6 unless an exact, safe representation is established.
- [ ] Implement whole-batch validation of ownership, charm slot type/unlock state, upgrade tier, prerequisite techniques and point accounting. Identify all genuine mirrored fields; report derived changes and restore the entire batch on failure.
- [ ] Add owned-gear presets, resource/ammo presets and narrowly scoped technique operations with affected-field previews. No broad story-unlock or “unlock everything” action.
- [ ] Validate equipment persistence across menu reopen, game reload, armor switching and another save; validate technique costs, usable abilities and continued progression. Test NG/NG+/Iki separately.
- [ ] Resolve any demonstrated sibling-file dependency using the existing multi-file output contract, with explicit conflict checks. Do not blindly mirror gameplay edits into unrelated slots or memory.

Evidence needed: R6/R7/R9/R10/R13/R14/R15. No SDK expansion blocks a small fixed-width, plugin-validated action. Exit: independently checked exact affected bytes, coherent undo and game-observed persistence per edit family.

**Phase 4 — World and collectibles, reads first. Goal: reliable missing-content tracking. Size L; risk medium for reads, high for writes.**

- [ ] Collect one-action shrine, fox-den, hot-spring, haiku, bamboo, pillar and quest captures; separate discovery/visit/completion/reward bits and downstream health/resolve/charm changes.
- [ ] Construct named, region-scoped read-only activity/quest tables; label unmapped IDs and distinguish base game/Iki/NG+ denominators. Quest stage semantics require more than a completed count.
- [ ] Verify world coordinates against several known positions; then add optional map presentation with permitted art/tiles and a list fallback. No teleport controls without collision/streaming/safe-location evidence.
- [ ] Enable only individually proven non-story state changes after reward/dependency research; destructive resets, endings, quest rewinds and blanket completion remain deferred.

Evidence needed: R2/R6/R16/R17/R18. Exit: known completed and incomplete locations resolve correctly without claiming rewards or quest completion from discovery flags alone.

**Phase 5 — Yōtei research track. Goal: a documented reader, then the smallest demonstrable edit. Size L; risk high; may run alongside Phases 1–4.**

- [ ] Obtain known non-zero Mon/resources/ammo before/after saves from the same build, plus manual/autosave/memory captures; identify which table, if either, changes with the observed balance.
- [ ] Determine the role of header word1, content length, checksum coverage and any inner integrity. Test competing hypotheses and retain all-zero placeholders as separate cases.
- [ ] Read actual non-zero gameplay records with conservative validation; keep zero-valued schema/catalogue tables distinct. Establish mode/build differences before assuming Tsushima semantics.
- [ ] Only after integrity and authority are proven, attempt one private small existing-value edit, load/resave it on PS5, and compare. Add corruption rejection only for established invariants.
- [ ] Adopt named data through a Yōtei-specific namespace and manifest, then repeat the owned-gear/progression stages; no automatic port of Tsushima caps or maps.

Evidence needed: R5/R6/R19/R20. Exit can legitimately be an improved read-only reader if write evidence remains insufficient; no schedule should promise a writable Yōtei plugin today.

**Phase 6 — Structural inventory and complete workflows. Goal: supported add/remove/unlock operations with recoverable history. Size L; risk very high; optional after preceding gates.**

- [ ] Establish enclosing counts/sizes, reference/ownership links, record order, uniqueness, fixed-container slack/overflow and actual integrity dependencies before inserting or deleting anything.
- [ ] Implement item acquisition/removal and unlocks only for proven item categories; reproduce required acquisition/reward side effects. A definition row is not an owned-item template.
- [ ] Provide reversible structural transactions/snapshots and a preview of collateral changes; stable logical identities must survive row rebuilds, undo and reopened views.
- [ ] Prove no-op preservation, changed-region accounting and in-game persistence across multiple independent saves; leave unsupported categories read-only.

Evidence needed: R13/R14/R15/R21. Until then, the finished useful editor edits existing state and offers read-only catalogues; it need not manufacture items to deliver value.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Implementation stays inside `plugins/SuckerPunch/`; SDK requests are recorded in its README and shared framework work is separately assigned. Do not add plugin-local hooks/tooling that conflicts with `AGENTS.md`; this research did not alter existing tooling.
- [ ] Every supported title/build/mode and friendly-ID mapping cites a source revision and private sample/observation evidence. “Present in bytes,” “owned,” “equipped,” “completed” and “rewarded” are not conflated.
- [ ] All 25 Tsushima and 8 Yōtei payload entries retain byte-identical no-op exports; independent-content coverage remains 12 and 3 until additional captures arrive. Unknown fields, padding, platform words and metadata survive unchanged.
- [ ] Existing resource/ammo edits continue to change only approved count words and checksum; both ammo copies remain coherent. A malformed/mismatched/locked/unknown record cannot be made writable through a stale view or a bulk action.
- [ ] Synthetic tests cover checksum overflow/bounds, ambiguous/truncated/duplicate records and unsupported builds; sample-dependent conformance skips when samples are absent. Exact-span assertions extend to every new edit family.
- [ ] Resource names and capacities have independent known-state observations; 32767 is never labelled a game capacity. Unsupported modes and unknown limits retain honest labels and restricted actions.
- [ ] New equipment/technique/world readers first match recorded PS5 menus and controlled pairs. Unmapped identifiers remain visible with raw IDs and Unknown/Inferred confidence.
- [ ] Existing-gear presets enforce owned/eligible slots and technique prerequisites, reject invalid batches atomically, report all derived changes and support exact undo/redo across lazy-view rebuilds.
- [ ] Equipment/point/upgrade edits survive game reload and another save without unexpected equipment changes, lost progress or unexplained normalization. Each promoted edit family has a recorded PS5 validation outcome.
- [ ] World tracking distinguishes discovery, completion and reward; named locations and denominators are build/content-scoped. No unvalidated story/endings/completion bulk write is exposed.
- [ ] Yōtei remains read-only until authoritative non-zero state, checksum coverage and an in-game load/resave test are established. Zero padding and two matching additive sums alone do not satisfy this gate.
- [ ] Structural edits remain unavailable until enclosing counts/sizes/reference links, lifecycle side effects, overflow behavior and recoverable history are proved; an unknown item is never silently dropped.
- [ ] Catalogue/asset manifests retain provenance, licence, locale and mapping confidence. No copyrighted game dump, uncleared image or incompatible third-party implementation is committed; plugins perform no runtime network/filesystem I/O.
- [ ] Table warnings/confidence/errors are visible without hovering; gear controls support keyboard use, ID/name search and unknown-value fallback. Large catalogues use bounded/lazy sections and virtualised tables rather than eager full trees.
- [ ] Export continues to use Core's existing verification and backup policy; additional sibling outputs occur only for demonstrated dependencies and conflicting open writers fail clearly. No unrelated slot/backup generation is silently replaced.
- [ ] Final implementation runs plugin conformance, format/semantics tests and relevant shared Core/UI checks outside this planning round. README distinguishes tested serialization, actual game observations and remaining hypotheses; only a human sets Stable.

## Open questions & risks

- Which version/build/mode produced each private snapshot? Stored v49 does not identify PC. Exact-size probing may need a researched compatibility envelope for future patches, but loosening it without evidence risks false claims.
- Are the many equipment/technique strings schema/catalogue entries, unlock state or live instances? What distinguishes repeated minor charms, equipment slots, upgrades, active and per-armor loadouts? The raw identifier scan establishes none of these roles.
- What exactly are Tsushima's five inventory words, including flags 1/3 and the last two unknown words? Are equal ammo counts inventory/current-held state or another mirror? Current paired updates follow matching public cheats, but controlled consumption/resave must establish behavior.
- Which resource IDs are unused, Legends-only or shared definitions? The 48-record table includes test/unused-like names. A campaign editor should not expose an invented usable item merely because its name starts with Ammo/Ingred.
- Can localized display keys be recovered from user-owned definitions, or must a curated mapping bridge the save string and runtime gear hash? Hash algorithm, byte order and collision/alias behavior are not established; localization hashes must not be assumed interchangeable.
- Are health/resolve/XP and technique points derived from ranks, collectibles or quest rewards? Skill resets and mythic unlocks can have downstream progression effects; no unrestricted numeric character sheet should precede dependency evidence.
- Do any edits require `memory.dat`, slot files or `param.sfo SUBTITLE` to change together? The framework can output multiple existing files, but the SDK's normal file lookup excludes system files; metadata editing needs an explicit policy if pursued. Difficulty/time writes are optional and currently deferred.
- The Yōtei 13/16 field, apparent end, checksum coverage and zero-counter table roles remain unknown. More same-pattern samples may strengthen a correlation without distinguishing hypotheses. A failed console load may reflect integrity, a bad semantic edit, wrong build or the outer restore workflow; use unchanged restore controls.
- Licensing of original hash attachments and game imagery remains a blocker for distribution, separate from availability of MIT extraction code. Fandom text rights do not automatically cover embedded in-game quotations or icons. Keep optional private data packs private and out of save exports.
- PC asset tooling supports research on PC files; no XPPS/game-definition/texture dump was available for validation. All archive/texture references were read as untrusted text. No source-advertised speed, completeness or repacking guarantee is adopted as a verified claim.
- Public “100%” saves may include hidden mods, account-bound state or simultaneous changes. Obtain consent/provenance and a precise observation log; never publish private saves. Platform authentication/outer save-manager work stays outside the gameplay parser.
- Yōtei's current publisher page includes later content/modes; the supplied two non-empty snapshots do not establish support for those builds. Region/content manifests must grow from samples, not from current marketing lists.
