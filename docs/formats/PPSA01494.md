Its [encryption notes](https://github.com/iyre/bl4-save-tools/blob/main/docs/encryption.md) describe account-derived AES-256-ECB, PKCS7, compressed YAML and appended Adler32/length. [glacierpiece/borderlands-4-save-utility](https://github.com/glacierpiece/borderlands-4-save-utility) is credited for original encryption research (licence not audited). None of this was demonstrated on the supplied PS5 blob. Do not reuse BL3 BogoCrypt.
