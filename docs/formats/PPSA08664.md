# PPSA08664 — Final Fantasy VII Rebirth

> **Historical reconnaissance — 2026-10-28.** Measurements and unknowns below belong to the
> original research pass. Later parsers, writers and policy decisions supersede its implementation
> leads; see [current plugin status](../PLUGINS.md) and each plugin README for supported behavior.

**Medium; FF7 linked chunks with a Rebirth schema.** [Catalogue](https://prosperopatches.com/PPSA08664) and ff7rebirth names confirm identity; SFO is a placeholder. Common state is 1056 bytes; numbered 000–010 gameplay files are 23,381,104 bytes.

Verified: RESD repeated four times at `0x10`; absolute next pointer `0xE4330` at `0x04`; words `0xE4320`/1 at `0x08/0x0C`. Gameplay chains contain **275 records including tail** at `0x164C460`, ending exactly at EOF `0x164C470`. Later LOSD representatives have version-like word 27 and content length 85,840, unlike Remake. Representative H=1.3904 indicates sparse structures rather than whole-file encryption. Demo agrees on initial fields/chain dimensions, not proven gameplay field offsets. No independent checksum was established.

[KingdomSaveEditor](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/KingdomSaveEditor) is GPL-3.0 and supports **Remake**, not Rebirth; its chunk model is relevant, fixed schema sizes are not. [Original PC Rebirth editor](https://www.nexusmods.com/finalfantasy7rebirth/mods/2072) lists extensive gameplay support and validation; reuse licence and PS5 applicability are unverified.
