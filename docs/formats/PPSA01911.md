# PPSA01911 — Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade

> **Historical reconnaissance — 2026-10-28.** Measurements and unknowns below belong to the
> original research pass. Later parsers, writers and policy decisions supersede its implementation
> leads; see [current plugin status](../PLUGINS.md) and each plugin README for supported behavior.

**Medium; Square Enix FF7 linked chunks.** [Catalogue](https://prosperopatches.com/PPSA01911), ff7remake names and SFO INTERmission labels identify PS5 Intergrade. `ff7remakecommon` is 1056-byte system/common state; numbered 9,434,752-byte files are gameplay, with separate INTERmission directories.

Verified: `00 00 01 00` at zero; u32 absolute next pointer `0x50010` at `0x04`; RESD repeated four times at `0x10`. Next record at `0x50010` repeats LOSD at +0x10. Representative 000 has **24 linked records including tail** at `0x8FF670`; its 16-byte tail starts `04 00 01 00` and ends exactly at EOF `0x8FF680`. Common repeats COSD at `0x10`. Sparse/low-entropy data argues against whole-file encryption. No additional checksum was established.

[KingdomSaveEditor](https://github.com/Xeeynamo/KingdomSaveEditor) (GPL-3.0) supports Remake PS4/PC; KHSave.LibFf7Remake/Chunks/Chunk.cs supplies the matching pointer/container model. PS5 schemas still need sample validation; no source was copied.

Next: implement bounded linked navigation with cycle rejection, then validate common/character/inventory structures. Preserve unknown chunks and padding. Share the walker with Rebirth/demo, **not Remake’s fixed sizes or field offsets**. No in-game field/edit validation is claimed here.
