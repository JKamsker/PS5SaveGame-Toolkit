# Item icons and official-description excerpts

Retrieved 2026-10-29 from individual **Game8** Final Fantasy XVI item articles.
**Square Enix** owns the game icons and game text; Game8 supplies source pages
and imagery. Licence notice: **Rights reserved by Square Enix and Game8; used
with attribution per owner decision 2026-10-29**.

92 exact-name records: 36 accessories, 31 materials, 7 consumables, 8 weapons,
5 belts and 5 vambraces. Each has one local F2 `assetId` and a short official
Description-table excerpt, up to 24 words; ellipses mark shortening. Original
identifier attribution, Inferred mapping confidence, warnings and unknown IDs
are retained. No capacities/effects, build availability or ownership are inferred.

Icons are aspect-preserving WebP conversions at most 64×64. The F2 asset dataset
pins image hashes. Exact article/image URLs, input/output hashes, dimensions,
lengths and excerpts are in the [media ledger](../../../research/game-assets.json).
See [processing notes and index sources](../../../research/game-assets.source.md).
The record description credits the exact article; F2/Sources attributes `game_icons`.
This media notice is separate from the Nenkai community identifier dataset's
2026-10-28 owner-policy notice. It does not relicense publisher media.
