# Ability icons and official-description excerpts

Retrieved 2026-10-29 from individual **Game8** Final Fantasy XVI ability articles,
linked through its [ability index](https://game8.co/games/Final-Fantasy-XVI/archives/409930).
**Square Enix** owns the game icons and game text. Licence notice: **Rights reserved
by Square Enix and Game8; used with attribution per owner decision 2026-10-29**.

48 icons cover the existing 48 reference abilities, including Leviathan and Ultima.
Each record has a local F2 `assetId` and a short official Description-table excerpt,
up to 24 words; ellipses mark shortening. No saved skill-index/AP/flag association
is introduced. Existing names, stages and AP annotations retain their separate
CC BY-SA 3.0 provenance and notices in `data.source.md`.

Icons are aspect-preserving WebP conversions at most 64×64. The F2 asset dataset
pins hashes. Exact individual article/image URLs, input/output hashes, dimensions,
lengths and excerpts are in the [media ledger](../../../research/game-assets.json).
See [processing notes](../../../research/game-assets.source.md). Record descriptions
credit each Game8 article; the Sources panel attributes `game_icons`. Ability
images remain behind the existing lazy spoiler gate. Publisher media and its text
excerpts are not relicensed under the wiki adaptation's CC BY-SA terms.
