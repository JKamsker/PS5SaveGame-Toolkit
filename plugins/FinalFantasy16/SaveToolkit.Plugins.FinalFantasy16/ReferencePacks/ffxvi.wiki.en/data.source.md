Modifications: version 1.1.0 has **234 records**: the original 205, plus 25 new
wiki-derived labels and 4 MIT upgrade labels. Of the 29 additions, 26 match
previously unnamed observed main IDs; three upgrades are not in the observed main
corpus. Categories, singular/plural variants and capitalization are normalized.
Upgrade suffixes are factual distinctions from identifier research attached to
wiki-sourced base labels, not generated by ID arithmetic. The museum alias
Hanged Man Signboard (research label The Hanged Man), identical Martelle Apples
labels at two separate IDs, and source spelling Flame of Rebirth are documented
per record. Every mapping carries **Inferred** confidence and an evidence note.
No wiki prose, Japanese translations, images, icons, caps or availability rules
are included. Missing labels are never generated from ID ranges or copied from
uncleared CSVs. The 41 observed gaps in this wiki pack are named by the separate
published-ID pack described above.

Nenkai's [published numeric ID research](https://github.com/Nenkai/ffxvi-modding/tree/4b5e7eb349d2decd776b60b0f2d0f2af7aaaac03/docs/resources/ids)
was consulted to research identifier associations. Its CSV facts are now retained
separately; they are not claimed to be licensed by the wiki. This pack is an independently
curated selection of wiki-derived labels and factual identifiers, not a copy of the
CSV dataset. Those joins remain **Inferred**, pending controlled gameplay checks.

Locale `en`; build-agnostic **labels only**. No assertion of PS5 patch, DLC or NG+
compatibility. 131/172 distinct observed retail main IDs and 2/2 demo IDs are named;
all other IDs, including 900000, remain explicitly unmapped. Zero stacks are retained.

Regenerate deterministically offline:
`python -I plugins/FinalFantasy16/research/build_catalog.py`.
The converter validates canonical/duplicate IDs and plain-text names, and hashes
LF UTF-8 JSON for F2. The host validates the embedded manifest and hashes again.
No runtime network or filesystem access is used.

The complete decision ledger for the original 67 gaps is
[round2-naming-audit.json](../../../research/round2-naming-audit.json): 26 named
with source/evidence, 41 Unknown without an embedded unlicensed candidate name.
