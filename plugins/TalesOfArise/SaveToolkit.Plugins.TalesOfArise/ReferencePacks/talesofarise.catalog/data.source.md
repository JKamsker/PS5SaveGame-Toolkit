# Tales of Arise catalog attribution

This embedded F2 pack is `talesofarise.catalog` version `1.0.0`, English locale, and is limited to title `PPSA02745`.

| Dataset | Source and licence | Scope and transformation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `items` | [ToA Consumables](https://aselia.fandom.com/wiki/ToA_-_Consumables), Aselia Wiki contributors, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) | 13 names and the broad `Consumable` category. Quantity limit 15 is the existing conservative plugin policy, not a claim of the game's effective cap. Numeric IDs 1–13 are observed in the local PS5 samples; only ID 1 is directly cross-checked as Apple Gel in the current evidence. |
| `equipment` | [Aselia weapon-list category](https://aselia.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Weapon_Lists) and linked Tales of Arise weapon pages, Aselia Wiki contributors, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) | A small name-only starter subset grouped by character. Symbolic keys are catalog identities, not claimed PS5 serialized IDs. No stats, ownership, compatibility, replacement, or acquisition semantics are embedded. |

The pack contains no game artwork, guide prose, code, or private saves. Every record repeats its source URL; the manifest repeats dataset-level attribution. The plugin falls back to raw numeric IDs when the pack is unavailable.
