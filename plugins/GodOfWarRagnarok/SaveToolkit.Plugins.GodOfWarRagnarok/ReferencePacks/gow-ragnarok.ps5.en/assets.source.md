# Ragnarök resource icon sources

Retrieved 2026-10-29 from [God of War Wiki resource pages](https://godofwar.fandom.com/wiki/Resources/GOW_%28Ragnar%C3%B6k%29).
The Chaos Flame and Frozen Flame source icons were resized outside the repository to 96x96
or less WebP. The pack keeps the existing resource hashes and descriptions; `assetId` only
adds a visual catalogue thumbnail.

The asset dataset is attributed as `Rights reserved by Sony Interactive Entertainment and
Santa Monica Studio; used with attribution per owner decision 2026-10-29`.
