# Ragnarök skill thumbnail sources

Retrieved 2026-10-29 from the [Leviathan Axe](https://godofwar.fandom.com/wiki/Leviathan_Axe) and
[Draupnir Spear](https://godofwar.fandom.com/wiki/Draupnir_Spear) pages. Five selected skill
showcase frames were converted outside the repository to 96x96 WebP thumbnails. They are
visual references; they do not establish saved skill hashes or unlock state.

The five files are embedded in `gow-ragnarok.assets.en`, not this skill-facts pack. Their
individual records, filenames and source pages are listed in that pack's
[asset sidecar](../gow-ragnarok.assets.en/assets.source.md). The Blades of Chaos page supplies
skill facts, but none of these five thumbnails.

The skill images retain Sony Interactive Entertainment / Santa Monica Studio attribution under
the owner decision 2026-10-29. The shared visual pack's dataset notice also credits MapGenie
for its map mosaic; it abbreviates the holders as `Sony/Santa Monica/MapGenie`, with full
publisher names in `author` and the asset sidecar.
