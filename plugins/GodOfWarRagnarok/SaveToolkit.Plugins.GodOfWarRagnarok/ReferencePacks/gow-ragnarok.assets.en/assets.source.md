# Ragnarök visual asset sources

Retrieved 2026-10-29. The files in this pack are compact presentation assets, not save
format evidence. Downloaded files were treated as untrusted images, validated with Pillow
in isolated mode, resized outside the repository, and copied into the embedded pack only
after the resulting PNG/JPEG/WebP headers were checked by the reference-pack loader.

The asset dataset uses `Rights reserved by Sony/Santa Monica/MapGenie; used with attribution
per owner decision 2026-10-29`, with full publisher names in `author` and below. The factual
descriptions remain explicitly visual references and do not claim saved ownership, unlocks,
or equipped state.

God of War Wiki contributors supply the image pages; publisher game assets are attributed to
Sony Interactive Entertainment and Santa Monica Studio, and the map mosaic to MapGenie.
The pack contains eleven catalogue records and eleven images: six gear/rune/map references and
five skill thumbnails. Short weapon descriptions also cite the
[PlayStation combat guide](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/god-of-war-ragnarok/god-of-war-combat-refresher-course/)
in their records. This is the image pack used by skill details; the skill-facts pack contains no images.
