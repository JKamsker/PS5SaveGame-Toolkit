Mandarin, DSSS and typed-stream behavior was independently implemented from
the supplied verified research toolkit and observations of the sample bytes,
with the following upstream Rust files consulted as algorithm references:
https://github.com/kvasszn/ree-save-editor
  src/save/crypto/mandarin.rs
  src/save/crypto/util.rs
  src/save/game.rs
  src/save/types.rs
  src/sdk/type_map.rs
No substantial Rust source or complete game-data dumps are distributed here.

Resources/HashNames.json contains a subset of class/field identifier facts
from that project's assets/pragmata/rszpragmata.json and strings_pragmata.txt,
filtered to hashes present in the supplied samples. Each identifier is
independently checked against MurmurHash3, seed 0xffffffff.

AES-OFB test vectors are from NIST SP 800-38A, Appendix F.4.1:
https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/38/a/final
