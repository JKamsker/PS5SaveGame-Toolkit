The original Round 2 metadata descriptions summarized source classification without
imported effect text or artwork. The later asset/text additions below supersede that
historical scope; MIT terms cover the GTC metadata, not the publisher/guide material.

Write-enable character XP curves and inferred Rebirth weapon-family grouping use the same
pinned MIT resource.ts datasets. Source sidecar: Resources/character-curves.source.md.
Parser/dependency staging is independently implemented; no downloaded code was executed.

Completion-round folio and crafting/minigame extracts use the same pinned template.ts,
with utils.ts consulted for the global folio shift override. Hidden flags, dynamic
offsets and duplicate addresses were excluded; Queen's Blood aliases were not duplicated.
These MIT candidate extracts contain no prerequisites, SP costs, coordinates,
game artwork or effect prose.
Source sidecar: Resources/spatial-research.source.md.

groups from pinned MIT resource.ts, with read-only enemy record layouts, five Rebirth
relationship words and one deliberately unclassified craftsmanship word from template.ts.
