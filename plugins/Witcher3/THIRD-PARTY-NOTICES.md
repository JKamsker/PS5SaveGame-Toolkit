# Third-party notices

## Witcher Wiki item reference data

`ReferencePacks/witcher3.starter.en/items.json` adapts factual labels, native IDs and categories from
[Witcher Wiki contributors](https://witcher.fandom.com/wiki/Category:The_Witcher_3_items).
Individual source pages are linked in every record. Retrieved 2026-10-28.
Licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

PS5 Save Game Toolkit contributors selected 18 mappings, normalized categories and wrote short
descriptions and uncertainty annotations. The adaptation remains CC BY-SA 3.0. Retain these notices,
article links and the share-alike license when redistributing or modifying this data. No wiki prose
passages, game artwork, scripts, letter text or proprietary resource dumps are bundled.
See the pack's `data.source.md` for the licensing policy and scope.
