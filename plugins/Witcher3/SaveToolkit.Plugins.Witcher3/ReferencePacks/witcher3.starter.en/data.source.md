Modifications by PS5 Save Game Toolkit contributors: selected 18 exact native IDs, factual English
labels and normalized categories; rewrote short explanatory descriptions; distinguished starter
variants and called out patch-sensitive or unresolved semantics. The source articles establish the
ID/name joins; all 18 IDs also occur in the supplied PS5 corpus. Mapping confidence remains Inferred
because the PS5 patch/locale is undocumented and these are cross-build community labels.
