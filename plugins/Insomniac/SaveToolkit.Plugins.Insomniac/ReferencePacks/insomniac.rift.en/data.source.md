offsets, defaults, prose and all EUPL generated C#. Added original uncertainty notes.
Schema widths are not PS5 offsets. Type ID joins are checked against real record headers.

Only three factual names/categories were selected. No wiki prose, game descriptions,
prices, limits, icons or other artwork are included. Wiki pages are retrieved-date
sources, not revision-pinned exports. Candidate path joins are inferred from internal
names in the PS5 samples; no config/localization dump independently confirms them.

Scope: PPSA01474, English labels, unknown game build. The pack supplies no ownership,
entitlement, difficulty, level, ammo-cap, wallet or planet-index rules. Unknown paths
and hashes remain visible, and Open(file) works without any reference context.
